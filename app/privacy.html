Elvora web app

Privacy & voice data

Your logs

  • Meals, water, sleep, your profile and your chat with Elvora are saved in this browser’s local storage on your device.
  • The web app has no account, no analytics and no server-side database. It sends your logs nowhere.
  • You can export everything as a JSON file or delete it all from the You tab.

Microphone and voice

  • The microphone turns on only after you tap the orb, and turns off when listening ends.
  • The app measures the microphone level only to animate the orb. Audio is not recorded, played back or stored by Elvora.
  • Speech-to-text is provided by your browser. In Chrome the audio is sent to Google’s speech service; Safari may use Apple’s servers or on-device recognition. Their privacy policies apply to that step.
  • Spoken replies use your device’s built-in text-to-speech voices.
