      Privacy & voice data

      • Everything you log stays in this browser’s storage on this device. Elvora’s web app has no account and sends your logs nowhere.
      • The microphone turns on only after you tap the orb, and turns off when you stop or Elvora answers.
      • Speech-to-text is done by your browser. Chrome sends the audio to Google’s speech service and Safari may use Apple’s. Elvora never records or stores audio.
      • Replies come from Elvora’s on-device coach, using your logged numbers.
      • Elvora is a wellness coach, not a medical service. For pain, illness or medical questions, see a clinician.
